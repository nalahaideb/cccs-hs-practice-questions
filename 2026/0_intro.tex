\section{Overview}

The following is an assortment of practice questions that range from \textbf{Beginner} to \textbf{Advanced}.
Note that the goal of this pdf is not to teach you programming -- that
would require a whole book.

The questions are not ordered in their respective categories, some may be harder than others and appear sooner or they may be easier and appear later. Beyond the given examples, they are left without specific test cases.

If you find a question doesn't look/sound right, you want clarification on something, or if you just want to talk about computer science feel free to message us on the
\href{https://discord.gg/gGbqfrSeMD}{CCCS High School Programming Contest Discord}
(\href{https://discord.gg/gGbqfrSeMD}{\texttt{https://discord.gg/gGbqfrSeMD}}).

Over the coming months before the programming contest (\emph{March 6th, 2027})
we will be posting solutions and walkthroughs for some of the questions in the
document.

It takes times to be a strong programmer or software engineer.
Be patient.
Good luck and have fun!
